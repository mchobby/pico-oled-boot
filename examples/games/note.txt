# 10 retro-games for arduino and OLED

- Asteroids – Navigate and destroy space rocks.
- Breakout – Classic paddle and ball brick-breaking action.
- Dino Run – Skip over obstacles in this endless runner.
- Flappy Bird – Avoid pipes with perfectly timed flaps.
- Maze Runner – Find your way through randomly generated mazes.
- Pacman – Eat dots and avoid ghosts in the classic maze.
- Pong – The original table tennis simulation.
- Snake – Grow as long as possible without hitting walls or yourself.
- Space Invaders – Defend Earth from waves of alien invaders.
- Tetris – Stack blocks and clear lines in this iconic puzzle game.
